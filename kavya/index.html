I’ve always believed every place has a story.

Some people collect places. I collect the stories in them.

I grew up on stories, like all of us. Nani-dadi ki kahaniyan, the same one every night, and nobody ever skipped. Somewhere along the way, content forgot how to tell them.

So I started KK Create to go find India’s stories, from every district: the longest trains, the smallest rooms, the biggest markets run by women. A phone on a desk became a team of 40+ and billions of views.

Now I want everyone to be able to tell the stories that matter to them. That’s why we teach. My students call me Kavya di.

That’s the tape so far.
