I’ve always believed every place has a story.

I grew up on stories, like all of us. Nani-dadi ki kahaniyan, the same one every night, and nobody ever skipped.

Somewhere along the way, content forgot how to tell them. So I started KK Create to go find India’s stories, from every district: the longest trains, the smallest rooms, the biggest markets run by women.

A phone on a desk became a team of 40+ people, a million-subscriber channel and billions of views. Brands like Netflix, Google and Swiggy came along for the ride.

Now I want everyone to be able to tell the stories that matter to them. That’s why we teach. And yes, my students call me Kavya di.
